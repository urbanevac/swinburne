3-Year Strategic Roadmap

The Discipline of Planning

"Failing to plan is planning to fail." — Benjamin Franklin
  • Establish institutional PMO governance built for local operational agility.
  • Safeguard campus infrastructure transformation and capital expansion investments.
  • Milestone baselines made visible — live RAG status, risk registers, and sprint progress tracked in a single PMO dashboard.

The Need for Agility

"No plan survives an encounter with the enemy." — Helmuth von Moltke
  • Adopt rapid Agile execution cycles to manage changing requirements.
  • Continuous tactical loops allowing pivots without bureaucratic delays.
  • Eliminate friction points to rapidly expand campus capacity targets.

FROM FOUNDATION TO VELOCITY

Year 1 • Stabilize

Agile Governance

Deploy a balanced Hybrid PMO. Protect infrastructure with Waterfall frameworks while running system digitizations and student portals through iterative Scrum Sprints.

Transparent value realization tracking - showing real business impact
Sprint Board visibility across all active workstreams — status known at a glance, every cycle
Year 2 • Empower

Growing Local Talent

Establish dedicated training and coaching tracks to upskill local campus operations. Pair this execution with active project "living labs" to simultaneously absorb student talent into delivery pipelines.

Structured coaching & upskilling for local operational staff
High-impact Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) student pathways
Year 3 • Expand

Accelerate at Scale

Leverage matured governance to seamlessly scale campus capability beyond 5,000 students without expanding administrative friction or baseline overhead constraints.

Portfolio-level benefits realisation — measurable ROI tied to every project milestone
Optimized resource allocation to shorten project delivery timelines

The Operational Engine Room

Flexible Tech Options

  • Tracking & Roadmaps: Jira Enterprise OR Asana/Trello/Clickup
  • Knowledge Hub: Confluence wikis OR Microsoft SharePoint ecosystems
  • Executive Dashboards: Power BI as primary pipeline — portfolio health, RAG status, and KPIs surfaced in real time

Project Structure

  • Strategic PM Lead: Direct lifecycle delivery.
  • Functional Scrum Team: Academic IT, Finance, Facilities & Vendor leads — each owning delivery accountability.
  • Sarawak Intern Pipeline: Driving campus-level WIL goals.

Stakeholder Strategy

  • Single Source of Truth: Eliminate metric variance.
  • Bi-Weekly Review Loops: Risk register triage + MVP value demos — issues escalated before they become delays.
  • Strategic Alignment: Map work directly to university goals.
